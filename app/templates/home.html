Why It Matters

The Journey from Data to Decision

Explainability is not a feature — it is the foundation. Here is exactly how Cardiac.AI turns raw health numbers into life-changing clarity.

Step 01

Patient Data Intake

Clinical indicators are entered — age, sex, chest pain type, resting blood pressure, serum cholesterol, fasting blood sugar, ECG results, maximum heart rate, and more. Inputs are validated and normalized via StandardScaler before model ingestion.

Step 02

Probabilistic Risk Scoring

The logistic regression pipeline evaluates each feature's contribution and produces a calibrated risk probability. Because the model is inherently interpretable, every coefficient is meaningful and auditable by clinical staff.

Step 03

SHAP Feature Attribution

SHapley Additive exPlanations decompose the model output into per-feature contributions. We identify which clinical factors are pushing the risk score upward or downward — and by exactly how much.

Step 04

LLM-Generated Plain Language Report

SHAP values are passed to a large language model alongside patient context. The result is a personalized, medically-grounded explanation in plain English — actionable for both the patient and their physician.
